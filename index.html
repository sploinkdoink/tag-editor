After you save: add it to Spotify

Pick your phone. Menu names can differ slightly between app versions.

1

In Spotify, open your profile picture, then Settings and privacy, then Local Files. Switch on Local audio files.

Profile picture›Settings and privacy›Local Files
Local audio files
2

Open the Files app and find your saved MP3 in the Downloads folder.

♪

Files › Downloads
3

Press and hold the file and choose Move (or Copy). Then go to Browse, open On My iPhone, and choose the Spotify folder.

Browse›On My iPhone›Spotify
4

Back in Spotify, open Your Library, tap the Playlists filter, and open Local Files.
